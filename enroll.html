International transfer

Studying from outside Pakistan? Our fees are small, so how you send matters more than how much. The cheapest route is usually a remittance service such as Wise, sent to the Meezan Bank account below. Next cheapest is a card payment. Message us and we will send you a secure link, which costs well under a dollar. A direct bank wire also works, but it typically costs 20 to 30 US dollars on its own, so it only makes sense if you are paying for several months at once. If you have a Payoneer account, you can send to ours free of charge.
